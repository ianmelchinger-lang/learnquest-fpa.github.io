Measure the gap between profit and cash

Profit and cash follow different calendars, so the gap between them needs a number. Two measures size it. You advise Sample Enterprise Manufacturing (SEM), a fictional manufacturer. Compute both measures for two quarters of one product line, then say which line moved most.

Operating cash flow as a share of net income divides cash from operations by net income. The cash conversion cycle counts the days cash stays tied up in operations. It adds days inventory outstanding, the average days goods sit before sale, to days sales outstanding, the average days customers take to pay. It then subtracts days payable outstanding, the average days the business takes to pay suppliers.
