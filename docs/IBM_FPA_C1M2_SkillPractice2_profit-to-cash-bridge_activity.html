Build the bridge from profit to cash

The statements share one set of numbers. Net income lifts retained earnings on the balance sheet, and it is also the first line of the cash flow statement. From there the cash flow statement converts profit to cash, and its ending cash must equal the cash on the balance sheet. You advise Sample Enterprise Manufacturing (SEM), a fictional manufacturer. Build the bridge for one product line, then test whether the links tie.

Retained earnings are the profit a business has kept since it began. Depreciation is the cost of equipment spread over its life. Receivables are money customers owe, inventory is goods held, and payables are money owed to suppliers. All figures are USD millions for one quarter of Product line 4.
