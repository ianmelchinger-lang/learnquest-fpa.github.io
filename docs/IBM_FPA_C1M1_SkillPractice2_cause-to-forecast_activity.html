Does the cause last? Update the forecast

Three instruments run the finance calendar. The budget is the approved annual target, and it stays fixed. The forecast is the live projection of where the year ends, and it changes when you learn something. A variance is the gap between plan and actual, and it is useful when it carries a cause.

You advise Sample Enterprise Manufacturing (SEM), a fictional manufacturer. Nine months are closed and three remain. Four cost lines are over plan. Read each cause note and mark whether the overage lasts. The year-end forecast moves as you mark.

All figures are in USD millions. A lasting cause adds its monthly overage to each month left. A one-time cause adds nothing. A cause no one has explained adds nothing to the forecast and shows as open exposure.
