Build the finding for one receiver

A correct number becomes a decision only when the person receiving it can act on it. You advise Sample Enterprise Manufacturing (SEM), a fictional manufacturer. Build a three-sentence finding for one receiver. Pick the sentence for each slot that this receiver can decide on.

The finding

Plant energy is USD 1.80 million over plan through month 9. The utility contract price rose in month 5 and stays fixed through year-end. Months 1 to 4 ran at plan. The forecast effect is USD 0.36 million a month over plan, USD 1.08 million across the 3 months left.

Fill all three slots to check.

Change the receiver
