Input, output, or diagnostic?

A KPI set that holds only results reports what already happened. Sorting KPIs by the job they do shows which ones move first. An output KPI measures a result the unit answers for, such as revenue, cost, or retention. An input KPI measures a resource or activity that drives results, such as headcount, volume, or spend. A diagnostic KPI measures how well the process between inputs and outputs runs.
