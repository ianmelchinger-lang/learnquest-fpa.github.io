Does the draft change a decision?

A finance draft earns its place when it changes what someone decides. You advise Sample Enterprise Manufacturing (SEM), a fictional manufacturer. Eight drafts are on your desk. Sort each one by what it can do for the decision it serves.

A decision owner is the person who makes the decision. A recommendation states what the owner should do. Days are counted from today.

Shapes the decision
The owner can use it before deciding, and it says what to do.
Arrives after the decision
The owner decides before the draft is ready.
Names no decision or no action
No decision is named, or the draft does not say what to do.

Place all eight drafts to check.

Move one decision date

Pick a draft and move its decision date. The draft stays the same. Watch where it lands.

SEM is fictional. All dates and drafts in this activity are invented for practice.
