Tactical or strategic request?

Finance work divides into two kinds. Tactical work supports decisions that middle managers and operational teams own, over a horizon of about one to two years. Strategic work supports decisions that the Chief financial officer and executive leadership own, over a horizon of about three to five years. The split is a division of labor, and each kind depends on the other.
