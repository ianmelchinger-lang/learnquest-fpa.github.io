Place each number in the cascade

The KPI cascade turns a strategic goal into numbers a team can act on, in three tiers. The goal sits on top. Beneath it sit financial targets, which show in accounting terms that the goal is being met. Beneath those sit operational KPIs, which are numbers a business unit controls that drive a financial target. A number belongs in the cascade only when it traces up to a target and points down to an action.
